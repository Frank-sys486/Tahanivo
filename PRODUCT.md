# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite with React, deployed on Vercel.

## Users

People looking for high-quality furniture with an exclusive, luxury presentation.

## Product Purpose

TAHANIVO enables customers to discover and purchase premium furniture online or request a quote when they need a tailored buying conversation. There is no physical showroom.

## Positioning

TAHANIVO offers exclusive furniture distinguished by top-tier craftsmanship and product quality.

## Operating Context

Customers evaluate furniture through the website, then either complete an online purchase or submit a quote request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Support direct online purchasing.
- Support quote requests.
- Do not present or imply a physical showroom.
- Product categories, catalog details, pricing, payment provider, fulfillment, and quote workflow remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: TAHANIVO.
- Position the brand around exclusivity, craftsmanship, quality, and luxury.
- Create a minimal abstract logo using a two-dimensional shape, not a letterform or monogram.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing logo, product photography, catalog data, testimonials, customer claims, pricing, or performance claims have been provided. Future work must not fabricate them as factual evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make craftsmanship and material quality easy to evaluate online.
- Present exclusivity through product substance and restraint, not unsupported claims.
- Keep purchasing and quote requests equally clear without implying a showroom visit.
- Preserve a premium experience across desktop and mobile.
