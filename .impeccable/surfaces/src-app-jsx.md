# TAHANIVO Storefront Surface

## Scope and mode

Homepage and storefront entry for a persuasive ecommerce experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Quality- and luxury-minded furniture customers evaluate the collection, purchase online, or request a quote. The interface must never imply a showroom.

## Chosen direction

The Living Alcove expressed through the approved Quiet Catalog composition at `.impeccable/mocks/living-alcove/option-c-quiet-catalog.png`.

## Memorable moment

A monumental close crop of one furniture piece meets a broad plaster field, separated by a single weathered-timber threshold. The three-stroke open-center mark anchors the navigation.

## Distilled hierarchy

The page has one primary path: view the collection, understand the craft, then request a quote. The hero carries one sentence and one action. Collection entries use only category and name.

## System inventory

- Ground: pale plaster with charcoal-green type and restrained cobalt actions.
- Components: flat controls, fine dark outlines, minimal corner rounding, no floating card chrome.
- Type: modern restrained sans, large quiet display scale, compact uppercase navigation.
- Imagery: tactile furniture close-ups, weathered timber, matte upholstery, plaster, and bronze.
- Motion: premium and controlled; quick feedback, slow image settling, no bounce.
- Responsive: collapse to one column while preserving product scale and open space.

## Removed complexity

- Removed journal content, collection search, disabled account and bag controls, repeated product descriptions, the hero paragraph, and the secondary hero action.
- Do not restore journal, search, account, or cart UI until real content or commerce services make them functional.
- White space and photography carry the luxury signal; new copy must earn its place.

## Non-literal elements

The generated chair, room, and collection crops establish composition and material direction only. They are not factual catalog products, prices, or claims.

## Open decisions

Real catalog data, prices, product photography, payment provider, fulfillment, and quote handling remain undecided.
