# TAHANIVO Icon Surface

## Scope and mode

Hidden brand presentation at `/icon`, intended as a direct-address showcase rather than a navigable storefront destination.

## Audience, job, and action

Visitors who receive the exact route see the TAHANIVO identity and its defining visual world immediately. There is no navigation or commercial action on this surface.

## Chosen direction

An editorial brand plate extending the Quiet Catalog: monumental open-center mark and wordmark on pale plaster, held against the storefront's defining furniture crop.

## Memorable moment

The mark settles into focus as the furniture image uncrops, leaving the brand and “Objects with presence.” as the only text.

## Constraints

- No link to `/icon` appears elsewhere in the storefront.
- Reuse the supplied mark, current hero image, established palette, and Hanken Grotesk.
- Preserve the full composition on desktop and mobile without adding controls or claims.
